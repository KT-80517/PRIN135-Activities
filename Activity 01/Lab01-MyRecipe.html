Cheesy Scrambled Egg Recipe

A simple recipe on how to cook a sunnyside up egg

Ingredients

  • Egg
  • Mayonnaise or Milk
  • Cheese
  • Pepper
  • Butter or oil

      • Instructions

      1. Whisk the egg/eggs into a small bowl
      2. Add a 1Tbps of mayonnaise and whisk it again
      3. Add some grated cheese into the mixture
      4. Warm your non-stick pan over low heat
      5. Melt your butter until it starts to foam
      6. Pour your egg mixture into the pan
      7. Wait for a couple of seconds for the egg to start cooking
      8. Gently push the eggs from the edges to the center using your spatula.
      9. Keep moving the eggs slowly until soft curds form.
      10. Remove the pan from the heat while the eggs still look slightly glossy and wet.
      11. add a dash of pepper
      12. Serve right away on a warm plate.
